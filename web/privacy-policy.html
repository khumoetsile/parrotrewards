Parrot Rewards privacy policy

Operator: Parrot
Privacy and support contact: kmolabi@gmail.com
Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice describes the customer app, customer website and supporting Parrot Rewards service. Parrot Rewards helps customers discover offers, save discount claims and, when chosen, submit purchases for receipt verification.

Information the service processes

  • Account information: your name, email address, account identifier, password hash and profile/notification preferences. The server hashes passwords; sign-in requires the password you enter.
  • Authentication information: access and refresh credentials needed to keep you signed in, and password-recovery records when requested. Mobile credentials use platform secure storage; browser credentials depend on your browser's storage and security.
  • Offers and claims: the offers you claim, claim references, saved prices and conditions, status, dates and provider-validation records.
  • Optional purchase verification: receipt photographs or files you choose to submit, purchase amounts, shop details, extracted receipt text, review history, disputes and associated verification records. Receipt content can contain personal information. Avoid submitting unnecessary card, identity or other sensitive details.
  • Promotional activity: campaign/referral identifiers supplied through promotional links and their association with your account or claims.
  • Operational records: request metadata, errors and security/audit records needed to run the service. Hosting infrastructure may process connection information such as IP addresses.

Why information is used

Information supports registration and sign-in, profile management, discount claims and provider validation, optional purchase verification, account recovery, support, abuse prevention and service administration. Camera or photo access is used when you choose a feature that needs it, such as scanning a shop code or selecting a receipt. Declining permission may prevent that feature; the app offers QR-text entry as an alternative to camera scanning.

Who can receive information

Authorized Parrot staff administer the service. Authorized provider staff can access claim and purchase information needed for their provider's validation/review workflow. Hosting, database/storage and email service providers process information necessary to operate these services. Account-recovery emails are sent to the account email address. The application has no configured advertising SDK or in-app payment gateway. Google Play and any external sites or email apps you choose to use operate under their own privacy practices.

Security

The app uses HTTPS for its hosted API, password hashing on the server, authenticated access to account records and platform secure storage for mobile sign-in credentials. Private receipt files are separate from public website files. These measures do not guarantee that every device, browser or network is secure. Do not share passwords or sign-in tokens in support/deletion messages.

Retention and deletion

Account, claim and receipt records currently have no automatic account-age expiry configured. They remain in the service until removed in response to a verified request or operational cleanup, subject to any specifically applicable retention obligation. Session and password-recovery credentials have validity limits; expiry does not itself mean their database records have been erased.

Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting Parrot from the email address used for your account. This is a manual request process. Parrot may verify ownership and will review what data can be removed. If particular records need to be retained for a legal, security or fraud-prevention reason, ask Parrot to identify those records, the reason and the applicable period. Account deactivation alone is not data deletion.

Backup copies may remain until the hosting backup cycle replaces or removes them. A fixed backup-retention period has not yet been confirmed; contact Parrot for the applicable hosting/backup details. Removal requests must also be considered if data is restored from backup. Sending a request does not instantly erase the account, and this notice does not promise a fixed deletion-completion deadline.

Your choices and questions

You can update supported profile details, change available notification preferences, sign out and decline optional camera/photo access. Contact kmolabi@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion, ask about providers/retention, or raise a privacy concern. Use a minimal description and your account email; do not include a password.

Changes

The date above identifies this notice's current version. Review the notice when the service's data practices change. Parrot must keep this notice and its Google Play disclosures consistent with the service actually provided.
