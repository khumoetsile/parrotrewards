Request deletion of your Parrot Rewards account

Operator: Parrot. You can request deletion without installing or opening the app.

  1. Email kmolabi@gmail.com, preferably from the email address used for your account.
  2. Use the subject Parrot Rewards account deletion request and identify your account email.
  3. Parrot may ask you to verify ownership, review associated records, and confirm completion or explain any specific retention requirement.

Open email request

If the button does not open an email app, copy the request below into your preferred email service and send it to kmolabi@gmail.com. Do not include your password or sign-in token.

To: kmolabi@gmail.com
Subject: Parrot Rewards account deletion request

I request deletion of my Parrot Rewards account and its associated data.
Account email: [enter your account email]

Please verify ownership, explain any records that must be retained and why,
and confirm when deletion is completed.

What the request covers

Your account/profile details and associated claims, receipt images and purchase-verification records. If particular records must be retained, Parrot should explain the reason and applicable period. Deactivation alone is not deletion.

What happens next

This is a manual process handled by Parrot. Opening an email draft does not send it; send the email in your email app. Sending a request does not immediately erase your account. Contact the same address for a status update. A fixed processing deadline and backup-retention period have not yet been confirmed.

Read the privacy policy for current data-use and retention information.
