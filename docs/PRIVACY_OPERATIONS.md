# Parrot Rewards privacy/deletion operations

Operator confirmed by the owner: Parrot. Public contact confirmed by the owner: kmolabi@gmail.com. Do not replace this with an invented support mailbox.

The app and public account-deletion page initiate a manual email request. They do not automatically delete, deactivate or modify production accounts. Never tell a customer that opening a draft means their request was sent or completed.

The public privacy URL registered in Play was https://parrotrewards.org/privacy-policy, but it returned the Flutter application shell. It must serve the new standalone HTML policy, not a sign-in page or PDF. The public request page is https://parrotrewards.org/account-deletion. Both are publicly accessible without an account.

## Processing a verified request

1. Monitor the confirmed mailbox. Verify that the requester owns the relevant account; do not ask for passwords. Record the request and keep access to it restricted.
2. Identify the user's account, refresh/recovery credentials, notification/profile records, claims and redemptions, campaign touches, receipts/images, purchase verification/OCR text, history, flags, jobs, disputes and relevant audit records. Consider related records and private files, not just the User row.
3. Determine whether any specific accounting/legal/security obligation genuinely requires retaining particular records. Inform the requester of the records, reason and applicable period. Do not invent a blanket exemption or substitute deactivation for deletion.
4. An authorized operator must carry out approved removal through a reviewed procedure and verify that private receipt files and active credentials have been removed/revoked, while preserving unrelated customers and shared provider/catalogue records. This release does not implement automated erasure or a safe production erasure command.
5. Confirm the outcome to the requester. Include any retained-data explanation. Keep a secure record of requests while backup copies could restore removed data and reapply verified removals after a restore.
